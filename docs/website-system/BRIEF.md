## Business

- **Name:** Dispatch
- **Location / service area:** Online web app, available everywhere

## What they do

> Hire AI agents by name: describe the job in plain English and the agent starts today
> Agents report to agents, and Dispatch routes every task to the right one
> Agents follow your manuals, use your apps (email, calendar, browser, your CRM) and keep a memory of the work
> Agents schedule their own follow-ups, finish jobs while you're away and email you the results
> You set how much they do alone: chat only, ask first, or fully autonomous, with one-click approvals
